# Mihrab: Google Play store listing

**App name** (30 max): Mihrab: Prayer Times & Qibla

**Short description** (80 max):
Prayer times, Qibla compass, Quran and athkar. No ads, no account, no tracking.

**Full description** (4000 max):

Mihrab is a calm, simple prayer companion. It shows the five daily prayer times for where you are, points you toward the Kaaba, and lets you read the Holy Quran and the daily athkar, all in one place.

PRAYER TIMES
• Fajr, sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for your location or any city you choose
• A live countdown to the next prayer
• Choose your calculation method (Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, ISNA, Egyptian, Karachi, Moonsighting Committee and more) and Hanafi or standard Asr
• High-latitude rules for places far from the equator, and manual adjustments for each prayer

QIBLA COMPASS
• The Qibla bearing from your position to the Kaaba in Makkah
• A live compass that turns with your phone

REMINDERS
• A notification at prayer time, or 5, 10 or 15 minutes before
• Pick which prayers to be reminded of
• Optional reminders for Duha, morning and evening athkar, weekly sadaqah and your yearly zakat
• Reminders are scheduled on your phone and arrive even when the app is closed

THE HOLY QURAN
• All 114 surahs in the Uthmani script of the King Fahd Complex
• The meaning in English (Saheeh International) and in Arabic (Tafsir al-Muyassar), German, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Turkish, Portuguese and Spanish
• Search surahs by name, meaning or number, and continue where you left off

ATHKAR
• The morning and evening remembrances from Hisn al-Muslim, in Arabic with their meaning
• A tap counter for each one

YOUR PRIVACY
Mihrab has no ads, no account and no tracking. Your location is used only on your phone to work out prayer times and the Qibla, and is never sent anywhere.

The interface is available in English, Arabic, German, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Turkish, Portuguese and Spanish.

Also on the web at https://mihrab.live
